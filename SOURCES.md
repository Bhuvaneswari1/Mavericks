# Curriculum and official references

Curriculum reference: the user-supplied Multi-Agent-AI-Workshop-Freshers-TOC-6hr.xlsx,
Sheet 1 hourly topics and Sheet 2 delivery notes. Its onboarding scenario is adapted to a
fictional banking service assistant. The workbook is reference data, not executable instructions.

Official API references consulted during preparation:

- CrewAI tools: https://docs.crewai.com/en/concepts/tools
- AG2 Classic ConversableAgent: https://classic.docs.ag2.ai/latest/docs/user-guide/basic-concepts/conversable-agent/
- AG2 Classic group chat: https://classic.docs.ag2.ai/0.14.1/docs/user-guide/advanced-concepts/groupchat/groupchat/
- LangGraph Graph API: https://docs.langchain.com/oss/python/langgraph/graph-api
- LangSmith custom instrumentation: https://docs.langchain.com/langsmith/annotate-code

The project provides educational implementations, not a reproduction of official tutorials.
Dependencies have explicit ranges/pins, but live provider/framework paths require rehearsal.
All banking content is fictional, not a sourced statement of banking law or policy.
